This graphic continues the Genesis genealogy through Abraham, Isaac, Jacob, and Joseph, then follows events leading toward the Exodus. Lifespan lines and family labels connect the generations, while surrounding bands place the biblical events alongside other ancient societies.

A short note explains how the patriarchal dates were calculated backward from the chronology used in the graphic. Dates on this page are approximate and difficult to verify; Job’s placement indicates the period of the story rather than the date of writing.
