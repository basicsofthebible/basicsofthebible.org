I created this graphic to bring together the messages to the seven churches in Revelation 2–3. A map locates the churches, while separate panels present selected passages from each message. A shared color key distinguishes favorable, unfavorable, and mixed assessments, making the messages easier to compare.

The graphic also includes brief summaries of interpretive frameworks and numerical symbolism that inform my reading of Revelation.

For an overview of the book’s visions and interludes, see [Structure of Revelation](/projects/structureofrevelation/). Both graphics and the accompanying presentation are available below.
